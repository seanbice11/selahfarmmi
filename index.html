What we plan to grow

Picked fresh, grown with care.

Selah Farm will start as a small market garden, growing with organic practices and selling directly to neighbors at farmers markets.

Vegetables

Salad greens, tomatoes, peppers, carrots, and more, chosen for flavor and for how well they grow in our region.

Culinary herbs

Fresh herbs grown for the kitchen, from basil and dill to parsley and sorrel.

Cut flowers

Seasonal stems like zinnias and cosmos, grown with organic practices to bring a little of the field indoors.

Rooted intentions

Selah: to pause, and to grow on purpose.

Selah is a word from ancient songs that marks a pause, a moment to stop and take things in. We want that rhythm in how we grow food: without rushing, and without shortcuts.

Between now and 2028, we’re learning: taking classes, visiting working farms, and looking for the right piece of land. We plan to grow with compost, cover crops, and organic practices.

Soil first

Healthy soil grows healthy plants. We plan to rely on compost, cover crops, and light tillage.

Unhurried quality

We’d rather grow a few things well than a lot of things badly, and sell them fresh.

Close to home

We want to know our customers by name and sell close to where the food is grown.

Looking for land

A few acres, and a good neighbor.

We’re looking to lease a quarter to one acre of tillable land, with water, in Macomb or St. Clair County. We’d care for it, keep it in agriculture, and put everything in a written lease.

If you have land, or you’re an experienced grower open to sharing what you know, we’d love to hear from you.
